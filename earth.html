Earth's Info

Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the only known celestial body that supports life. Its unique position in the habitable zone allows the presence of liquid water, which is essential for living organisms. About 71 percent of Earth’s surface is covered by oceans, while the remaining area consists of continents, islands, mountains, plains, and deserts. The planet has a solid crust, a semi-molten mantle, and a dense core that generates a strong magnetic field, protecting it from harmful solar winds. Earth’s atmosphere is composed mainly of nitrogen and oxygen, along with traces of other gases that help regulate temperature and sustain respiration. This delicate balance of physical and chemical conditions makes Earth exceptionally suitable for life.
Earth is a dynamic planet that constantly changes due to natural processes such as plate tectonics, erosion, volcanic activity, and the water cycle. It rotates on its axis once every 24 hours, causing day and night, and revolves around the Sun in about 365 days, resulting in seasons. The planet supports an immense diversity of life forms, ranging from microscopic organisms to complex plants and animals, all interconnected through ecosystems. Earth’s climate system, driven by solar energy and atmospheric circulation, influences weather patterns across the globe. Human civilization has developed by utilizing Earth’s natural resources, but this also places responsibility on humanity to protect and preserve the planet for future generations.
